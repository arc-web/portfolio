## Public Inclusion Rule

Include:

- portfolio positioning;
- public profile copy strategy;
- exact image and video dimensions;
- public-safe proof categories;
- summary-level transcript counts;
- outreach strategy and approval rules;
- website authority hub structure.

Exclude until reviewed:

- raw subtitle archives;
- raw transcript chunks;
- account IDs, login notes, credentials, or credential paths;
- private Gmail, Fathom, Discord, proposal, or client-system details;
- unapproved attendee faces, client names, dashboards, videos, or voice samples;
- named client metrics unless the exact public use is approved.
